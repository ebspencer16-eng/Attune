Per-dimension signal
Mean score and outlier rate — how often a dimension's own reading disagrees with the person's overall axis. High outlier % flags a dimension that fights the type.

Construct validity
Item-rest correlation: each dimension vs the rest of its own axis, and vs the other axis (Pearson r, spectrum-oriented). A dimension should track its own axis and not the other. Low own-axis r or a high cross-axis r flags a dimension that may be on the wrong axis or adding noise. This is the empirical check behind the weight choices.
