Your privacy choices

Attune Relationships LLC

Two of the choices below are already made for you, because Attune does not do the things they would turn off. They are here so you can see that, not to pad the page.

Nothing to opt out of

Sale of your personal information

We do not sell your personal information, to anyone, ever. There is no setting because there is nothing to switch off.

Nothing to opt out of

Advertising and tracking

We run no advertising trackers and no analytics trackers. This site sets no cookies. Nothing about you is shared for targeted advertising.

Your choice

Research use of de-identified answers

When an account is deleted we normally keep a de-identified copy of the exercise answers and profile details, with no name, email, or invite code attached. We use it to improve the exercises.

Turn this off and nothing is kept when you delete your account.

Sign in to set this. Your choice is stored against your account, so we need to know whose account it is.

Checking

Global Privacy Control

Checking whether your browser is sending the signal.

If your browser sends Global Privacy Control, we read it on our servers and record it as an opt-out of research use against your account. You do not have to do anything else. Turning the signal off later does not switch your opt-out back on. You can change it here.

Everything else

To ask what we hold about you, or to delete your account and your answers, write to hello@attune-relationships.com from the address on your account. We answer within 45 days, and complete deletions within 30 days.

If you want nothing kept at all, turn off research use above before you ask us to delete.
